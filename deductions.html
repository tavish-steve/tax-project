Kenyan Payroll Deductions Guide

Kenyan payroll deductions are calculated based on your gross salary. Here's how each deduction works:

NHIF - National Hospital Insurance Fund

What is NHIF?

NHIF provides health insurance coverage for Kenyan citizens. It's a mandatory deduction that ensures access to healthcare services at participating medical facilities across Kenya.

NSSF - National Social Security Fund

What is NSSF?

NSSF is a pension scheme that provides retirement benefits to Kenyan workers. It helps employees save for their retirement years and provides benefits in case of disability or death.

Contribution Rates
Contributor Contribution Rate Maximum Amount
Employee 6% of pensionable pay KES 1,080/month
Employer 6% of pensionable pay KES 1,080/month
Total 12% KES 2,160/month
Important Details:
  • For salaries above KES 18,000: Maximum contribution of KES 1,080 per month
  • For salaries below KES 18,000: 6% of gross salary
  • NSSF contributions are tax-deductible

NHDF - National Housing Development Fund (Housing Levy)

What is NHDF?

The National Housing Development Fund (also known as the Housing Levy) was introduced in 2024 to support affordable housing initiatives in Kenya under the Big Four Agenda.

Contribution Rates
Contributor Rate
Employee 1.5%
Employer 1.5%
Total 3%
Example:
If gross salary = KES 50,000
Employee contribution = KES 750
Employer contribution = KES 750
Total monthly = KES 1,500
Note: NHDF contributions are also tax-deductible and reduce your taxable income.

PAYEE - Pay As You Earn (Income Tax)

What is PAYEE?

Pay As You Earn (PAYEE) is the direct tax deducted from your salary by your employer on behalf of the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA). It is a progressive tax system where higher incomes are taxed at higher rates.

Tax Bands (Monthly)
Taxable Income (KES) Tax Rate Tax Amount (KES)
0 - 24,000 10% Income × 10%
24,001 - 32,333 25% 2,400 + (Income - 24,000) × 25%
32,334+ 30% 4,483.25 + (Income - 32,333) × 30%
Personal Relief
KES 2,400 per month - This is automatically applied to reduce your tax liability.
How Taxable Income is Calculated

Taxable Income = Gross Salary - Allowable Deductions

Where allowable deductions include:

  • NHIF contribution
  • NSSF contribution
  • NHDF (Housing Levy) contribution
Pro Tip: If your taxable income after deductions is below KES 24,001, you will pay KES 0 tax after applying personal relief!
